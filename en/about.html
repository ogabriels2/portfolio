From visual work to digital products

I started with design and audiovisual production. My Multimedia education taught me to organize information, compose interfaces and consider how people understand a message. Studying IT brought that perspective into software decisions.

I founded the Força Aliada community in 2021. In November 2025 I began its technology work: Manager for server management, Community, and services connecting accounts, products and support. I lead product direction, priorities, integrations, infrastructure, review and testing, with project collaborators.

With Miguel Downloader, I turned a practical need into a Windows application. I defined requirements and oversaw persistent queue, media analysis and file validation work. The academic projects HyroChat and Busca Preço add a different experience: building in a team, reviewing solutions and making the work understandable through the presentation.

How I work with AI

I use Claude Code and Codex during implementation. My responsibility is to define the problem, guide decisions, review code and verify workflows through testing.

At Força Aliada, this includes backend and security decisions, interface review and integration checks across the site and app. AI supports implementation; product choices and validation remain part of my work.
